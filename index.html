Online form builder FAQ

Everything you need to start collecting useful responses with Murdilimax Forms.

Can I create online forms for free?

Yes. Murdilimax Forms offers a free preview for creating, editing and publishing forms. You can start with an email address and a one-time sign-in code. The free preview currently supports up to 30 forms per account.

What types of forms can I make?

Build contact forms, surveys, event registrations, feedback forms and lead capture forms. Add short text, paragraph, email, numbers, website URLs, dates, dropdowns, multiple-choice questions and checkboxes.

Do respondents need to sign up?

No. Once you publish a form, anyone with its public link can fill it out and submit answers without creating a Murdilimax Forms account.

Where can I see form responses?

Responses are saved to your private workspace. Sign in to review submissions for each form and export your results as a CSV file.

How do I share a published form?

After publishing, copy the unique form link from your editor and send it by email, messaging or social media. Anyone with the link can open your form.
