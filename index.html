Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Can I create forms for free?

Yes. You can get started for free and create up to 30 forms in the current free preview. Sign in with an email code — no password needed.

Can people respond without signing up?

Yes. Anyone with a published form link can submit answers without an account.

What kinds of questions can I add?

Add text, paragraph, email, numeric, website, date, dropdown, multiple-choice, checkbox and file-upload questions. The upload limit is 2 MB per file.

Can I create multi-page forms with conditional logic?

Yes. You can split your questions across up to 10 pages and set a condition to display a question when an earlier answer matches your chosen value.

How do I collect and export responses?

Sign in to view answers in your private workspace. You can export the results to CSV.

Is Murdilimax Forms related to Tally?

Murdilimax Forms is an independent form-building service. We believe making a form should feel simple and enjoyable.
