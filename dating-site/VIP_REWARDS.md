# VIP reward pilot (26 November 2026)
No payments, premium functions or countdown are enabled. Ledger stores calendar months independently of referral days. Current campaign includes existing qualifying member-owned profiles created before midnight Riga 1 Jan 2027, one reward per account. Admin/archived listings alone do not qualify. Referral attribution is one-time at new account insertion, from a 30-day first-referral cookie. Verified email, complete approved member profile with stored photo, 72 hours and two active UTC days are evaluated automatically every 15 minutes. Human review is required before a 5-day referral award because published profiles and Telegram accounts do not prove identity. Shared IP, exact repeated sanitized photos, repeated email and optional Telegram linkage are risk signals, not proof. IP digests are keyed and retained for 90 days; collection limited to referral participants. Telegram linkage endpoint requires configured bot token and actual Mini App integration; no token is committed. Gifts transfer days, not calendar months; no negative gift balance or self-gift, idempotent transfer IDs, global transaction lock. Fraud moderation supports revocation and blocking confirmed involved accounts; no automatic ban on IP. An email verification flow is provided separately from normal login, delivery depends on Firebase. Before production: confirm backup of actual QDE database, validate proxy trust, run isolated PostgreSQL integration and full CI, test authenticated user and admin flows, review privacy notice and legitimate-interest/retention assessment. Premium activation requires later explicit release.
