# MurdiliMax Points (MP) v3
One MP = one future VIP day; campaign is a fixed 180 MP (six 30-day months). Existing historical campaign and reversal entries are retained; all balances use SUM(days + 30*months) and gifts debit that same unified ledger. No balance migration or duplicate grant. Campaign eligibility: account registered before 2027-01-01 Riga time, legacy unknown joined_at treated as historical, no bans or known duplicate-IP eligibility; after rollout new accounts need an eligible observed IP. Existing awards preserved. No photo requirement for the campaign; referrals still require a new account, complete approved adult profile with stored photo and unique eligible IP, +5 MP. Automatic evaluation after session/profile creation, dashboard refresh, and every minute. Gifts can spend all MP, including campaign, by selecting a recipient with a public member profile; no recipient code necessary. VIP redemption not live. Future proposal: 30 MP for 30 VIP days, atomic debit plus entitlement; no implementation until explicitly launched. Cash redemption is under consideration only, no rate, obligation, sale, withdrawals or cash feature. Before any cash launch, determine legal/regulatory/tax obligations, funding and fraud controls. Admin authentication unchanged; no payment services added.
