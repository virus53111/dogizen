Safer dating starts with respect

MurdiliMax helps adults meet across European cities. Protecting personal details, keeping control of your photos and reporting suspicious behaviour can make online connections safer.

Email verification is not identity verification

A verified email proves access to an address, not that a profile's identity or photos are authentic. Don't send passport photos or sensitive documents to a match. If someone's story changes or feels suspicious, stop and report it.

Avoid payment and romance scams

Be cautious if a new contact urgently requests money, crypto, gift cards or travel payments. Do not send funds to someone you have only spoken to online, even when they promise a meeting, relationship or emergency repayment.

Messaging, blocking and reports

Use the Report control on any public listing, including admin-published profiles. Describe the problem rather than sharing unrelated personal information. Block unwanted contacts in private chat. Messaging requires the profile's real owner to take control of their account.

Plan a safer first meeting

Choose a public venue, arrange your own way home and tell someone you trust where you will be. Never feel obliged to share your address, documents or devices. You may leave at any time.

Photos and personal information

Only upload photos of consenting adults. Newly uploaded images are reprocessed to remove embedded location metadata. Avoid exposing a home address, employer, private phone number or habitual schedule in public profiles.

Access your data and request deletion

Sign in and open My data & privacy to download a JSON copy of your own records or request account deletion. Deletion requests require separate review and are not immediately executed. Reports of unlawful content can also be submitted through the listing.
