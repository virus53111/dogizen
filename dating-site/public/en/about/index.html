How to use MurdiliMax

MurdiliMax is a dating and social directory for adults. Profiles should represent people who actually agreed to be listed, not invented members. Search by country and city, save interesting profiles and connect.

Finding profiles in your area

Open the directory and choose a country, city and category. You can narrow your search by age or only show profiles with photos. If the results are limited, broaden the filters. We do not generate pretend members to fill an empty search.

Creating a profile

Sign in using Google or register with an email address. Email verification may be required to protect other members. You must be at least 18 and use your own details and photos you have permission to publish.

Free messages and favourites

Private messages are free after sign-in: no paid subscription is required. You can also use the community chat and save profiles. Imported legacy profiles cannot receive new messages until their owner has been verified.

Safety and consent

Never send money, passwords or identity documents to someone you have just met. Choose a public location for the first meeting. Report suspicious profiles and block unwanted messages.

Growing a real community

This is a developing service, so availability depends on real people choosing to publish profiles. Invite adult friends to participate if they wish, but never publish their photos or personal details without consent.

Dating preferences

Choose “No financial expectations” for everyday dating, or “Supportive relationships” to discuss mutual support. Paid sexual encounters and advertising sexual services are prohibited. Dating preferences.
