Privacy & optional site analytics

Updated 31 October 2026. We measure visits to the public marketing website murdilimax.com only after you agree to optional analytics.

What is recorded?

Page path, time of visit, referring website's domain (if available), and a random session identifier stored in your browser's session storage. No name, email, full referrer URL, IP address or advertising identifier is written to our analytics database. No third-party advertising or Google Analytics cookies are used.

The data is transmitted to the Murdilimax Forms application at murdilimax-forms.hatchable.site, where it is stored in a private PostgreSQL database for up to 90 days. Access to visit reports is restricted to project administrators. Session counts are estimates, not precise counts of individual people.

Your choice

Analytics are off unless you choose “Allow analytics”. You can refuse or change your choice at any time below.

Other data

The separate Murdilimax Forms application has its own data-processing practices for accounts and form submissions. This notice covers analytics on this public marketing site only.

Browser requests and hosting infrastructure may process technical connection data necessary for operating the site even if you decline optional analytics.
