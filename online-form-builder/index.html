How to create an online form in five steps

A good form starts with one decision: what information do you genuinely need? Keep the form focused, especially when people will answer it on a phone. Murdilimax Forms is a browser-based visual editor, so you do not need to write HTML or install software.

  1. Open Murdilimax Forms and sign in with an email code. The current free preview lets you create up to 30 forms.
  2. Start a new form and give it a specific title. Tell respondents why you are asking the questions.
  3. Add fields such as short text, paragraph, email, number, date, dropdown, multiple choice or checkboxes.
  4. Preview the form, test the questions yourself, and publish it when it reads clearly.
  5. Copy its public link and send it. People can reply without creating an account.

Choose question types that make answers usable

Use an email field for an email address, multiple choice when you need one selection and checkboxes when several choices make sense. A paragraph field is better than a one-line field for feedback. Dates, numbers and website fields help keep responses consistent.

If the subject requires context, split a longer form into multiple pages. Murdilimax Forms supports conditional display of questions based on earlier answers, so irrelevant follow-up questions can stay hidden. Do not request sensitive personal details unless you truly need them.

What happens after someone responds?

Responses are saved in the private workspace. You can review the answers in the dashboard and export them to a CSV file for further analysis. Forms can also accept supported small image and PDF attachments, with a 2 MB per-file limit in the current version.

For workflows, you can send response events to an HTTPS webhook. Integrations with services such as Make, Slack or Zapier can be built around webhook endpoints; direct native connections to every third-party service are not available.

When an online form is the right tool

A contact form helps visitors send questions. A registration form gathers event attendance details. A customer survey reveals what is working and what needs improvement. A simple internal request form can prevent information being scattered across chats.

Start with a short version and test it with a real respondent. Remove optional fields that do not help you make a decision. An understandable form normally gets more useful answers than one with too many questions.
